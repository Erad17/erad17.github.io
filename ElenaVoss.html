About the Artist

Elena Voss (b. 1978, Santa Fe, NM) is a contemporary American painter whose work navigates the liminal space between abstraction and observed landscape. Working primarily in oil on linen, her paintings evoke geological time — layered surfaces that suggest erosion, memory, and the quiet violence of natural processes.

Trained at the Rhode Island School of Design (MFA, 2004) and the Skowhegan School of Painting & Sculpture, Voss has exhibited internationally at institutions including the Denver Art Museum, the Norton Museum of Art, and the Ogunquit Museum of American Art.

She maintains studios in Hudson Valley, New York, and Marfa, Texas, where the vast desert landscape continues to inform her evolving practice.

Artist Statement

"I paint the space between what I see and what I remember. Each canvas begins with an observation — a ridgeline at dusk, the way ochre bleeds into shadow — but the finished work belongs to neither place nor moment. It is a record of attention itself, a topography of looking."
