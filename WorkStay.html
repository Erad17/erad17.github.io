        Our story · est. Pittsburgh

        Built in the gap between "assigned" and "housed."

        WorkStay started with a nurse asleep in her car outside UPMC Presbyterian — assigned Monday, housed nowhere, running on hospital coffee. We figured Pittsburgh's traveling clinicians deserved a landing pad, not a scavenger hunt. So we built a marketplace where the first question is never "how's the light?"         What we won't budge on

        Four standing orders

        01

        Proximity is healthcare.

        A four-minute commute is a clinical intervention. Sleep, recovery, and showing up sharp all start with how far the door is from the ward.

        02

        Furnished means furnished.

        Bed made, Wi-Fi on, coffee in the cabinet, blackout curtains hung. You should be able to arrive post-shift with one suitcase and exhale.

        03

        No 12-month traps.

        Contracts are 13 weeks for a reason. Housing should flex with the assignment — never the other way around.

        04

        Humans answer the phone.

        7 a.m. to 11 p.m. ET, a person picks up. After that, the platform keeps working so we can sleep — we understand the feeling.
