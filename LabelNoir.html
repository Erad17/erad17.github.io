The House — Manifesto

Seams you never see.

Label Noir keeps unusual hours and exacting standards. We are a small atelier of engineers and art directors who believe a website, like a jacket, should be judged by its seams — the ones you never see. Every build is cut to order, basted in motion, and pressed until the layout holds its shape under any light, on any screen, over any connection.

We work in seasons, not sprints. Each commission receives a pattern book, a motion budget, and a principal who answers correspondence personally. What it does not receive is a theme, a template, or a tracking pixel.

“Most of the internet is prêt-à-porter. We decline the comparison.” — The House, Season IX

The Pattern Book — Six Crafts

The Atelier

Six crafts, practiced in-house and cut to order. Each is documented in the pattern book; none is sold off the rack. Open a row for its specification.

  1. Bespoke Next.js and React builds engineered like tailored garments — seamed, lined, and finished to zero-CLS perfection.

    Signature — Hardware-accelerated motion budget enforced on every route.

    Delivered as — Next.js build · zero CLS · Lighthouse ≥ 95.

  2. Editorial art direction for digital launches — casting typography, pacing reveals, and setting the season's visual grammar.

    Signature — Moodboard-to-manifesto pipeline delivered as a living style plate.

    Delivered as — Living style plate · casting sheet · seasonal grammar.

  3. Tokenized component libraries cut like sewing patterns — every spacing value, serif scale, and inversion rule documented and reusable.

    Signature — Ink/paper inversion engine baked directly into the token layer.

    Delivered as — Token library · inversion engine · usage doctrine.

  4. GSAP-choreographed scroll narratives, liquid fades, and hover states that behave like fabric under the hand.

    Signature — Scroll-driven SVG ribbon pathing as the signature house motif.

    Delivered as — GSAP choreography · ribbon pathing · 60fps budget.

  5. Grain fields, liquid textures, and light-on-satin shader studies rendered at a steady 60fps.

    Signature — GPU-safe grain composite that never taxes integrated graphics.

    Delivered as — GLSL surfaces · grain composite · GPU-safe compositing.

  6. Audits and refits for existing sites — trimming bundle weight, pre-processing AVIF imagery, and pressing layout shifts flat.

    Signature — Sub-second LCP guaranteed on mid-tier mobile hardware.

    Delivered as — Audit report · AVIF pipeline · sub-second LCP.

Method — Three Fittings

How a commission is cut.

I.

Measure

A fortnight of questions, audits, and chalk marks. We leave with your dimensions, not your deposit.

II.

Baste

The first fitting: loose seams, honest motion, everything provisional so that everything can still move.

III.

Finish

Buttonholes, press, final reveal. Delivered with documentation and a standing invitation back to the salon.

Nothing here is sold off the rack. Bring us the difficult thing.
