Field Notes · Koroška, Slovenia

A valley that rides back.

Meet the crew →

The Drava carved this valley; the Karawanken guard it; and somewhere between the two, an electric motor makes the whole thing feel personal. We keep our fleet at a trailhead five minutes off Route 430 — tuned every morning, charged to 100% overnight, and handed over with a chalkboard briefing on today's surface conditions.

No queues, no plastic tourism. Just Luka, a map with too many pencil lines on it, and the best gravel in the Eastern Alps. Most riders come for a loop; most come back the next day for a bigger one.

    The Basecamp.

    People ask which bike is best. Wrong question. The right question is: which valley do you want to see by lunch?

    — Luka · Fleet Manager & Trail Guide

    Alpine Volt Fleet started in 2019 with four Drava Cruisers in a hay barn and a hand-painted sign. The barn got a roof repair; the idea didn't change: small fleet, big valley, zero rush.

    Today we're a founding member of the Koroška Bike Trail Network, a Bosch service partner, and the reason a surprising number of people in three countries now own e-bikes they first rode here.

    2019

    Four Cruisers, one barn, a chalkboard for conditions. First season: 212 riders.

    2021

    Bosch eBike Systems partnership — on-site diagnostics and genuine parts.

    2023

    Cargo fleet arrives. School runs, picnics and two-kid hauls become electric.

    2024

    Founding member of the Koroška Bike Trail Network — 240 km signed and mapped.

    2025

    Summit Pro limited series: twelve hand-numbered frames, Koroška paint.

    Trailhead FAQ.

    July and August, yes — the fleet sells out most mornings. The rest of the season, a day's notice is usually plenty. Walk-ins are welcome in shoulder season, but the good sizes go first.

    Helmets (XS–XXL), locks, chargers and a route briefing are included with every rental. Gloves are €5, and we stock rain shells you can borrow if the Karawanken change their mind mid-ride.

    Almost nobody runs flat — the Range Lab above exists precisely for that. If it happens anyway, switch to Eco and pedal (they're still good bikes), then call the trailhead number on your rental card and we'll come get you. Rescue is free within our trail network.

    The Cargo Hauler carries two child seats under the rain canopy, and we have tag-along trailers for the Drava Cruiser. Kids under 12 ride free on the river path route card.

    Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. If we cancel for weather — which happens maybe twice a season — you get a full refund or a rain-check with a free upgrade.
