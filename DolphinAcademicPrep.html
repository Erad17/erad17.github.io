§ Our Method

We don't sell panic. We build runway.

Dolphin Academic Prep was founded on a simple observation: families don't need more worksheets — they need a visible plan, a reachable coach, and paperwork that doesn't eat Sunday night. That's the whole system.

Step 01

Diagnose

A 90-minute academic intake covers transcript review, a full-length practice assessment, and — most importantly — a conversation about what the student actually wants. We map strengths before we map gaps.

Step 02

Architect

Your coach writes a term-by-term plan with milestones, test dates, and weekly loads. You eSign it in the Family Portal, so everyone — student, parent, coach — is literally on the same page.

Step 03

Coach

Sessions run 1-on-1 or in pods of four. Adaptive quizzes recalibrate difficulty after every answer, and live video problem-solving means homework never stalls for a week on one stuck question.

Step 04

Measure

Monthly score reports land in the portal with coach commentary. Parents get a plain-English summary; students get the next three targets. Plans bend with the data — never with hunches.

✦ Inside the Family Portal
