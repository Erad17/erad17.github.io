Our Approach

Our mission

Evidence-based care should never be a luxury.

Jacobs Neurobehavioral Center exists to make the most effective treatments in mental health — PCIT, ERP, and thorough psychological assessment — accessible to the families of Los Angeles who need them most, regardless of income or insurance.

We are clinicians first: we follow the research, measure outcomes, and tell you honestly what's working. And we are neighbors: we keep our waiting room calm, our language plain, and our fees within reach.

Help us keep it that way

"Natural light, clear sightlines, nothing cluttered — everything in its place."

What guides us

Three commitments, every session

Evidence over opinion

We use treatments with the strongest research backing and track progress with data — so you always know what's working.

Warmth as method

Safety isn't a nicety — it's a prerequisite for change. We move at your pace and never ask you to white-knuckle it.

Access as principle

Sliding-scale slots, bilingual care, and a no-turnaway promise for children in crisis — because need, not means, decides who we serve.

Our method

The treatments, in plain language

01PCIT

Parent-Child Interaction Therapy

For children 2–7. You play with your child while a therapist coaches you live through an earpiece — building cooperation and calm in weeks, not years.

02ERP

Exposure & Response Prevention

For OCD and anxiety. Graduated, supported exercises that teach the brain the alarm is false — reclaiming hours, rituals, and peace of mind.

03ASSESSMENT

Psychological Evaluation

Comprehensive testing that turns confusion into a clear diagnosis and a concrete plan — with school and workplace accommodation guidance included.
