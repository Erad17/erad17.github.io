Our Story

Crafted by Hand,
Made with Heart

Founded in 2009, Terra & Kiln began as a one-woman studio nestled in the Pacific Northwest. What started as weekend pottery sessions evolved into a beloved artisan brand, known for pieces that blend organic forms with contemporary design.

Every piece we create is wheel-thrown and hand-glazed using locally sourced materials and non-toxic, eco-friendly glazes. We believe that the objects we use daily should bring joy, character, and a connection to the earth.

How It Works

From Clay to Your Home

Every piece goes through a meticulous, hands-on process — from raw clay to finished art.

01

Selection

Browse our collection or request a custom piece with your preferred glaze, shape, and size.

02

Crafting

Each piece is wheel-thrown by hand, then refined over multiple sessions to achieve the perfect form.

03

Firing & Glazing

Pieces are bisque-fired, hand-dipped in eco-friendly glazes, and fired again at 2300°F.

04

Delivery

Carefully packaged in recycled materials and shipped to your door with complete peace of mind.
