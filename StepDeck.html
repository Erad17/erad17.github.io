DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Software that pushes back.

Most browser instruments feel like demos. StepDeck is built like a faceplate: every control has weight, every action has an LED, and every knob already knows its CC.

01

Knob-for-knob, LED-for-LED

The Hardware Mirror keeps screen and silicon in lockstep. Turn the real thing and watch its on-screen twin glide. Drag the twin and watch the physical ring light.

02

Thirty seconds to first sound

Plug in, auto-map, play. The onboarding is the instrument — there is no onboarding. If you're not playing a fat unison bass in half a minute, we've failed.

03

Color-coded, like the greats

Orange is synthesis. Blue is drums. Green is the grid. Your hands learn the map before your eyes do — the same trick every great groovebox ever taught.
