Our Story

Real Vegetables.
Bold Flavors. Zero Compromise.

Dizza was born from a simple frustration: why are pizza toppings limited to the same tired condiments? We set out to create something entirely different — finishing drizzles made from real, roasted vegetables and premium oils that transform any slice into a gourmet experience.

Unlike traditional tomato sauces cooked onto your dough, Dizza drizzles are designed to be added after baking, preserving the vibrant color, complex flavors, and nutritional goodness of real vegetables in every drop.

🌱

Made from Real Vegetables

No artificial flavors. Just roasted, blended perfection.

🔥

Drizzle After Baking

Preserve maximum flavor and color with post-bake application.

Premium Ingredients Only

Cold-pressed oils, artisan spices, zero fillers.
