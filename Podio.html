The Agency

We don't manage careers. We build institutions.

Pódio was founded on a contrarian idea: that a management firm should represent fewer athletes, and do more for each one. While agencies chase volume, we spend seasons watching one athlete before we ever extend a contract — then we build around them with a small, senior team that answers the phone.

Our roster spans the pitch, the Atlantic, the grid and the track — four disciplines, one philosophy. Performance first. Narrative close behind. And every partnership measured, never improvised.

Method

Three moves. No shortcuts.

01

Scout

We watch the athletes others haven't found yet — youth academies, regional heats, test days. Signatures come after seasons of observation, never before. If we approach you, it's because the data and the eye test already agree.

02

Build

Positioning, media training, contract architecture, language coaching. Each athlete gets a personal dossier — a living document of performance, story and commercial value — maintained by their dedicated manager.

03

Elevate

Sponsorships that fit the person. Appearances that respect the calendar. Placements that move the career forward, not just the invoice. When a Pódio athlete enters a room, the room already knows why.
